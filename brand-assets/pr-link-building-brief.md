# Switch Books — Link Building & Digital PR Brief

A brief for a freelance/agency link-building or digital-PR specialist. Please
read section 1 first — the anonymity constraint is non-negotiable and shapes
everything.

---

## 1. About the brand (read first)
- **Switch Books** is a free service that matches UK small-business owners with the right local accounting firm. Strong Sussex / South East focus, but serves the whole UK.
- Website: **https://www.tryswitchbooks.co.uk**
- **The owner is anonymous, and must stay that way.** No personal name, no trading address, no phone number, no Google Business Profile anywhere public. Every tactic must preserve this. If a link or placement would expose the person behind the brand, it is off the table.

## 2. The objective
- Earn **high-quality, relevant backlinks** to grow domain authority and organic rankings.
- Secondary: referral traffic and brand mentions/entity signals.
- **Not** the goal: volume of cheap links, or anything that risks a Google penalty or exposes the owner.

## 3. What's already done — please don't re-bill for this
The site is technically sound and well-optimised on-page. A specialist should focus on **off-site links**, not redo the below:
- Fast, mobile-friendly Next.js site; clean crawl/index; Core Web Vitals healthy.
- Structured data (Organization, Article, Breadcrumb, FAQ, Service), XML sitemap, IndexNow live, verified in Google Search Console **and** Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Strong on-page: titles/metas within limits, topical internal-linking clusters, guardrails enforcing quality.
- Content already published: a 9-article "switching accountants" cluster, plus cost/limited-company/bookkeeper-vs-accountant/self-assessment/payroll guides, and service, location and sector landing pages.
- **Linkable assets ready to promote:** a definitive "Complete guide to switching accountants" pillar + a downloadable one-page checklist.
- Brand profiles live: LinkedIn, X, Product Hunt (Crunchbase to follow).

## 4. Hard constraints (non-negotiable)
- **Anonymity:** author under a pseudonym or "the Switch Books team" only. Never the owner's real name, address or phone.
- **No Google Business Profile or local NAP citations** (they require a verifiable real address).
- **No links to/from the owner's other businesses or associated firms.**
- **White-hat only.** No PBNs, no bought link networks, no spun/AI-spam content, no mass directory blasts, no comment/forum spam. A penalty would be catastrophic for a young domain.

## 5. Target link types (priority order)
1. **Editorial links from guest articles** on UK small-business, finance and Sussex/South-East business publications (pseudonymous byline). Highest value.
2. **Digital PR** — a data/opinion angle (e.g. commentary or a small survey on switching accountants, hidden fees, or what SMEs want from an accountant) pitched to journalists via a named "Switch Books spokesperson". Earns news links.
3. **"Resources / useful links" placements** on relevant blogs and small-business sites.
4. **Brand-only directories & listings** (startup/fintech/SME directories that take brand + URL only, no personal details).
5. **Unlinked brand-mention reclamation** — find existing mentions of "Switch Books" and request a link.

## 6. Target niches / publications (to research, not guaranteed)
- UK small-business media and blogs (e.g. small-business.co.uk, startups.co.uk, business-community sites).
- Personal-finance and accountancy-adjacent blogs.
- Sussex / South East regional business news and directories.
- Founder/entrepreneur platforms that accept contributor articles.

## 7. Assets available to leverage
- The switching-accountants pillar guide + checklist (built to be cited).
- All existing articles for repurposing into guest angles.
- Bespoke guest articles can be written to order (the brand has a dedicated content resource) — so a specialist can focus on **placement**, not writing from scratch, if preferred.

## 8. What to avoid (walk-away red flags)
- "We'll submit your site to 500 directories."
- Fiverr/bulk link packages, PBNs, link networks.
- Comment/forum/profile spam, irrelevant foreign-language sites.
- Anything requiring the owner's real identity, address or a Google Business Profile.

## 9. Deliverables & KPIs to expect
- A **target list signed off before outreach** — no black-box work.
- Monthly: a stated number of **quality links from real, named sites**, with each site's authority (DR/DA) and topical relevance, plus the live URL and anchor.
- Approval of any content before it goes live (protects the pseudonym and brand voice).
- Reporting: links earned, referring-domain growth, and any referral traffic.
- Prefer **placement/performance-based** pricing over a vague monthly retainer.

## 10. Budget guidance
- Quality editorial/guest links typically ~£150–£500 each; a digital-PR campaign £1,500+.
- **Start small:** a handful of genuinely good placements beats a big-volume package. Scale what works.

## 11. Vetting questions for the specialist
- "Show me 3 links you've built recently for a UK client, on real sites I can visit."
- "How do you build links for a brand with **no** Google Business Profile and **no** named owner?"
- "Walk me through your process — outreach, guest posts, PR. Do you ever buy links or use link networks?" (If yes → decline.)
- "Will I approve the target list and the content before anything goes live?"
